⚠️ NOT for manual download, install only via CurseForge app ⚠️

**Install this and everything is voiced.** It holds no audio and no code — it is a few kilobytes that names every Spoken addon and every English sound pack as a dependency, so your addon manager fetches the lot.

## What it installs

| Addon | Voices | Its audio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Spoken Player](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-player) | player | – |
| [Spoken Quests](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-quests) | quest dialogue and NPC gossip | [Spoken Quests Audio: All](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-quests-audio-all) |
| [Spoken Zones](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-zones) | zone and subzone lore | [Spoken Zones Audio](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-zones-audio) |
| [Spoken Books](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-books) | books, letters, notes and plaques | [Spoken Books Audio](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-books-audio) |

## Language

This is the English set. Other languages have their own sound packs, to be installed separately.
